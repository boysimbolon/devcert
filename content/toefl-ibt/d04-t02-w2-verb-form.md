## Kenapa skill ini diuji
Setelah subjek-verba, soal Build a Sentence paling sering menguji bentuk verba: tense, pasif, dan verba setelah preposisi/modal.

## Konsep inti
Penanda waktu → tense:

| Penanda | Tense |
|---|---|
| yesterday, last week, in 1990 | past simple |
| since, for, already, yet | present perfect |
| by the time, before | past perfect |
| tomorrow, next week | future |
| every day, usually | present simple |

Bentuk yang sering salah:

```text
Pasif     : was submitted   (bukan was submit)
Modal     : must submit     (bukan must to submit)
Preposisi : interested in learning (bukan in to learn)
Perfect   : has not yet finished (urutan baku)
Paralel   : to review, to edit, and to publish
```

## Jebakan
- Dua verba utama tanpa konjungsi ("The report was written submitted").
- *Since* + present perfect, bukan past simple.
- Lupa bentuk ketiga: *have went* seharusnya *have gone*.
- Pasif pada verba intransitif ("was happened").

## Latihan
1. "She ___ (work) here since 2019."
2. "The proposal ___ (review) by the committee last week."
