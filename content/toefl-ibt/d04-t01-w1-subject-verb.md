## Kenapa skill ini diuji
Ini skill Structure paling sering diuji — dan paling sering muncul di Build a Sentence. Kesalahannya selalu sama: subjek tertutup frasa penyela.

## Konsep inti
Langkah:

1. **Buang** frasa penyela: *along with*, *together with*, *as well as*, *including*, *which/who clause*.
2. Temukan subjek inti.
3. Cocokkan verba: tunggal → *is/was/has*; jamak → *are/were/have*.

```text
Salah: "The list of items were attached."
Inti : The list (tunggal) -> was
Benar: "The list of items was attached."

Salah: "Each of the students have a form."
Kata jumlah: each/every/one of -> tunggal
Benar: "Each of the students has a form."
```

Aturan cepat:

| Subjek | Verba |
|---|---|
| each, every, one of, neither, either | tunggal |
| both, few, many, several | jamak |
| the number of | tunggal |
| a number of | jamak |
| news, mathematics, physics | tunggal |

## Jebakan
- Mengikuti kata benda terdekat (*of items* → *were*).
- Menganggap *a number of* sama dengan *the number of*.
- Melewatkan subjek berupa frasa gerund ("Reading books **is** helpful").
- Lupa bahwa *data* sering dianggap jamak dalam teks akademik.

## Latihan
1. "The report, together with the appendices, ___ (was/were) submitted."
2. "A number of students ___ (has/have) complained."

<details><summary>Jawaban</summary>
<p><b>1.</b> <code>was</code> — subjek inti "the report" tunggal; frasa "together with" tidak mengubahnya.</p>
<p><b>2.</b> <code>have</code> — "a number of" diikuti verba jamak.</p>
</details>
