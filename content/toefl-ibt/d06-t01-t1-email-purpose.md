## Kenapa skill ini diuji
Penilai melihat apakah email mencapai tujuannya. Email yang sopan tetapi tidak jelas tujuannya kehilangan poin pertama.

## Konsep inti
Struktur tiga paragraf:

1. **Sapaan + tujuan** — siapa kamu, mengapa menulis.
2. **Detail** — tanggal, alasan, lampiran.
3. **Penutup + tindakan** — apa yang kamu harapkan.

```text
Subject: Request for extension on assignment 3

Dear Professor Lee,

I am writing to request a short extension for assignment 3, which is
currently due on Friday, 14 March. I have been unwell since Monday and
have a certificate from the campus clinic.

I have completed the first two sections and expect to finish the analysis
within four days. I would be grateful if the deadline could be moved to
Wednesday, 19 March.

I can provide the certificate if needed. Thank you for considering my
request.

Kind regards,
Rani Putri
```

Register yang tepat:

| Terlalu informal | Tepat |
|---|---|
| Hey prof, gotta ask… | Dear Professor Lee, I am writing to… |
| Need extension now. | I would like to request an extension. |
| Thanks! | Thank you for your consideration. |

## Jebakan
- Pembukaan panjang tanpa tujuan ("I hope this email finds you well. I am a student who…").
- Nada terlalu informal atau terlalu kaku berlebihan.
- Lupa subjek email yang informatif.
- Menutup tanpa menyebut tindakan yang diharapkan.

## Latihan
1. Di kalimat keberapa tujuan sebaiknya dinyatakan?
2. Perbaiki: "Hey, I need more time for the essay."
