Docker Certified Associate

Container tingkat associate: swarm & service (25%), image & registry (20%), instalasi & konfigurasi (15%), networking (15%), security (15%), storage (10%).

Kode ujianDCA
Jumlah soal55 soal
Durasi90 menit
Nilai lulus65% (sumber lain 66–67%)
Format13 pilihan ganda + 42 penilaian opsi per opsi
Bobot domain25/20/15/15/15/10
0 dari 40 topik
Mulai ujian latihanSoal pilihan ganda, ada timer dan pembahasan.

Rencana belajar

  1. Pekan 1

    Dasar container, image, Dockerfile, dan build cache.

  2. Pekan 2

    Registry, tag/digest, multi-stage build, dan content trust.

  3. Pekan 3

    Swarm: init, join, service, replica, dan stack Compose.

  4. Pekan 4

    Rolling update, secret, node label, healthcheck, dan kuorum.

  5. Pekan 5

    Instalasi, daemon.json, storage driver, logging driver, akses TLS.

  6. Pekan 6

    Networking: bridge, overlay, macvlan, publish port, DNS internal.

  7. Pekan 7

    Security: seccomp, capability, AppArmor/userns, mTLS, rootless.

  8. Pekan 8

    Storage: volume, bind, tmpfs, NFS, backup-restore, lalu ujian latihan.
